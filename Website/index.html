About Me

Every nerd has an origin story.

       I built my first x386 computer when I was 10 and my first html website at 12. Since then, my journey has led to a fascination with the rapid development of technology, instilling a passion for learning and a drive to understand how it all works.

       After serving our military and completing college, I sought out every experience I could. Always the first volunteer to take on new projects and learn new technologies, I honed my skillset. This drive and ambition led me to a career as a system administrator, network engineer, private & public cloud administrator, and most recently into the realm of DevOps , IaC, and solution architecture.

       I've worked with organizations and businesses covering the entire spectrum. From small family-owned busnesses to Fortune 100 companies, all have had something of value to teach me, and all have propelled my career.

        Never satisfied with my abilities and always moving towards my next goal, I continue to pursue new opportunities and develop my skill-set. I look forward to interacting with new peers, mentoring and learning from others, and expanding the scope of my abilities in the realm of technology solutions.
